# Jerseys

Nico's football jersey collection and wishlist, shared with family.

## Language

### Teams

**Team**:
A football side that has Kits. A Team is a Club or a National Team.

**Club**:
A Team that plays in one League.
_Avoid_: Franchise

**National Team**:
The Team of one Country.
_Avoid_: Selection, country (alone)

**League**:
The top competition of one Country. A Country has one League. A Club's League is its current one: relegations are not tracked.
_Avoid_: Ligue, championship

**Country**:
A place that has a League and a National Team. It is on one Continent.

**Continent**:
One of Europe, Asia & Oceania, Americas, Africa.
_Avoid_: Region, confederation

### Collection

**Kit**:
One design of a Team: a Team, a Season and a Kit Type. Jerseys are of a Kit.
_Avoid_: Maillot, shirt, model

**Main Picture**:
The picture of a Kit that all its Jerseys and its Wishlist Item show. A Kit can also have more Pictures.
_Avoid_: Cover, artwork, photo

**Season**:
When a Kit is worn. A National Team's Season is one year (2024). A Club's Season follows its League's Season Format.

**Season Format**:
How a League counts Seasons: Season (start and end year, 2023-24) or Calendar (one year, 2024).

**Kit Type**:
Which kit of the Season it is: Home, Away, Third, Fourth, Cup or Special.
_Avoid_: Type (alone)

**Version**:
The cut of a Jersey: Fan, Player or Tracksuit. One Kit covers all Versions.
_Avoid_: Edition

**Jersey**:
One physical item of a Kit that Nico owns. It has a Version, a Size, a Fit, and can have a Print.
_Avoid_: Maillot, shirt, item

**Collection**:
All Jerseys that Nico owns.

**Coverage**:
Which Countries Nico owns a Jersey from. A Country is covered when Nico owns a Jersey of its National Team or of a Club in its League.

**Print**:
The name and number on a Jersey, written as on the shirt, in any script (NICOLAS 4, 尼古拉斯 4).
_Avoid_: Flocage, flock

**Size**:
The labelled size of a Jersey: S, M or L.

**Fit**:
How a Jersey fits Nico: Too small, Good or Too big.

**Acquisition**:
How a Jersey came to Nico: a Purchase or a Gift.

**Purchase**:
An Acquisition where Nico paid a price, in euros, at a Store. It has a Bought date, and can have a Sent date and a Received date. Any of these dates can be a year only.
_Avoid_: Order, achat

**Gift**:
An Acquisition where someone gave Nico the Jersey, on one date. It has no Store and no price, and can name the giver.
_Avoid_: Cadeau

**Store**:
A shop or agent where Nico buys Jerseys. "Official Store" is one Store: the shop of whichever Team it is.
_Avoid_: Shop, vendor, seller

### People

**Owner**:
Nico, the only person who changes the Collection and the Wishlist. The Owner logs in with a password.

**Viewer**:
A family member who looks at the Collection and the Wishlist and changes nothing.
_Avoid_: User, guest

### Wishlist

**Wishlist**:
All Kits that Nico wants and does not own yet.
_Avoid_: WL

**Wishlist Item**:
A Kit that Nico wants, maybe in a given Version. The Wishlist lists them by Season, newest first.

**Got it**:
Nico gets a Wishlist Item: it leaves the Wishlist and becomes a Jersey.
_Avoid_: Buy, convert, move

**Drop**:
Nico no longer wants a Wishlist Item: it leaves the Wishlist and does not become a Jersey.
_Avoid_: Delete, remove

**Delete**:
Nico removes a Jersey, Kit, Team, Country, Store or Picture entered by mistake. It is refused while other rows use it. A Wishlist Item is never deleted: it is dropped.
_Avoid_: Remove, drop
